# Privacy Policy — OBS Scripture Dock

**OBS Scripture Dock** does **not** collect, store, track, or transmit any personal data, usage analytics, or telemetry.

- **Local Storage:** Your preaching playlists, display preferences, and Bible selections are stored strictly locally on your computer via Qt `QSettings`.
- **Network Access:** Network requests are made **only** when you explicitly click the CrossWire SWORD downloader (`🌐`) to fetch the public Bible module catalog and download public Bible texts from `crosswire.org`.
- **Third-Party Sharing:** No data is shared with the developer or any third party.
